# **What am I fighting for?**
~~Fighting for food, money and awards?~~
## It seems that someone has forgotten what they are fighting for.
In China. I've had so many chances to win my own awards. I don't care about that.
I don't think you have to win lots of awards to be successful. Internationally, no one cares what awards you've won; they only care about one thing.

## **"Who are you fighting for?"**
For your homeland? For the land you live in? Or for yourself?
I give myself a brilliant reason:"I won so many, but I lost too."
I don't want to lose my lover, my sanity, my health or my friends just to win so many awards.
I dreamt of hundreds of thousands. I don't. That's a **nightmare**.

## **So, Just keep your passions.**
**Write** because you love it; **run** because you love the wind; **create** because it makes you feel alive.
The world will always try to put a price on your efforts and measure your worth by the number of trophies you have won. **Let them**.
Pure passion doesn't need an audience. All it needs is you to keep it burning.
